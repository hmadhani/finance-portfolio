Closing fills: price the closing legs with `price-spread` using the opposite actions (closing a short = BUY) and the position's own qty, and use its fills. The closing run's `entry_cost_per_share` has the CLOSING trade's sign (+ = you pay, − = you receive). The CLI reports only `fees_round_trip` for the trade it prices: entry fees = the opening `price-spread`'s `fees_round_trip` / 2 (recorded in the OPEN ledger entry); exit fees = the closing `price-spread`'s `fees_round_trip` / 2. Then:
- realized P&L ($) = −(close.entry_cost_per_share + position.entry_cost) × 100 × qty − (entry fees + exit fees)
- cash += −close.entry_cost_per_share × 100 × qty − exit fees
- If the closing `price-spread` exits 2 (no quote / no valid quote for a leg), do not close: hold, log the gap in the Daily Decision Log, and retry next cycle. Expiration and assignment settle at intrinsic and need no `price-spread`.

Assignment (a CSP's short put ITM at expiration): replace the put leg in positions.json with shares — keep the `id`, set `strategy` to `assigned_shares`, legs = `[{"action":"BUY","ratio":1,"option":"STOCK"}]`, `entry_cost` = strike − credit received per share, `qty` = contracts, `max_loss` = −(strike − credit) × 100 × qty. Cash −= strike × 100 × qty (the credit was already added at entry). From then on NAV counts the shares through the mark (`value_per_share` = spot). Write an ASSIGNED ledger entry.

Covered calls — only on shares from assignment:
1. Contracts ≤ shares held / 100, and — because every leg in a positions.json entry is multiplied by that entry's `qty` — the call is written on ALL of the share position's lots: contracts N = the share position's `qty`. Use this run ONLY for analysis: it must show `defined_risk: true`, and its `max_loss` is the combined stock+call position's max loss for the 3% and 25% checks in STEP 4.
